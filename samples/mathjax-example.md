# MathJax Example

## Inline math

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$, and Euler's identity is $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

A sum with a subscript, $x_i$, and a limit, $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} = 0$.

## Display math

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

## Matrices

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
ax + by \\
cx + dy
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## Aligned equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x) &= (x + 1)^2 \\
     &= x^2 + 2x + 1
\end{aligned}
$$
